What's on the Exam

    One-sided limits, limits at infinity, L'Hôpital's Rule, 3 conditions for continuity

    Power, product, quotient, chain rules · implicit differentiation · trig/log/exp derivatives

    Related rates, optimization, curve sketching, particle motion, MVT

    Riemann sums, u-sub, FTC 1 & 2, area between curves, accumulation

    Slope fields, separation of variables, exponential growth/decay
  • BC: Series & More
    Sequences, convergence tests, Taylor/Maclaurin series, parametric, polar
