AP Calculus AB / BC

Limits, derivatives, integrals, and series. AB covers the core; BC adds parametric, polar, and sequences.

📅 Exam: May ⏱ 3 hr 15 min 🔥 Difficulty: Hard ~60% pass rate
⚡ Exam Tips
  • FRQs are 50% of your score — show every step, even if unsure
  • Memorize derivative + integral rules cold before exam week
  • L'Hôpital's Rule only works for 0/0 or ∞/∞ — check first
  • BC students: start series early, it's its own world
  • Use your calculator for definite integrals, intersections, numerical derivatives

📚 Core Topics
What's on the Exam
  • Limits & Continuity
    One-sided limits, limits at infinity, L'Hôpital's Rule, 3 conditions for continuity
  • Derivatives
    Power, product, quotient, chain rules · implicit differentiation · trig/log/exp derivatives
  • Applications of Derivatives
    Related rates, optimization, curve sketching, particle motion, MVT
  • Integrals & FTC
    Riemann sums, u-sub, FTC 1 & 2, area between curves, accumulation
  • Differential Equations
    Slope fields, separation of variables, exponential growth/decay
  • BC: Series & More
    Sequences, convergence tests, Taylor/Maclaurin series, parametric, polar
