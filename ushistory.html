📚 The 9 Periods
APUSH Timeline
  • Period 1 (1491–1607)
    Indigenous societies, Columbian Exchange, early European exploration and contact
  • Period 2 (1607–1754)
    English colonial regions, development of slavery, salutary neglect, colonial economies
  • Period 3 (1754–1800)
    French & Indian War, Revolution, Articles of Confederation, Constitution debates
  • Period 4 (1800–1848)
    Market revolution, Jacksonian democracy, Manifest Destiny, reform movements
  • Period 5 (1844–1877)
    Sectional crisis, Civil War causes, Reconstruction amendments and failures
  • Period 6 (1865–1898)
    Industrialization, Gilded Age, Populist movement, immigration, labor unions
  • Period 7 (1890–1945)
    Imperialism, Progressivism, WWI, Great Depression, New Deal, WWII
  • Period 8 (1945–1980)
    Cold War, Korean & Vietnam Wars, Civil Rights Movement, Great Society
  • Period 9 (1980–Present)
    Reagan Revolution, end of Cold War, globalization, 9/11, contemporary politics
