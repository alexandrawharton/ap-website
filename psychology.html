AP Psychology

The science of behavior and mental processes. More vocabulary-heavy than calculation-heavy — concepts, researchers, and classic studies matter most.

📅 Exam: May ⏱ 2 hours 🔥 Difficulty: Medium ~65% pass rate
⚡ Exam Tips
  • Negative reinforcement is NOT punishment — it removes something bad to INCREASE behavior
  • Know your landmark studies: Pavlov, Skinner, Milgram, Asch, Bandura, Harlow
  • FRQ 1: apply multiple concepts to a scenario — one clear sentence per concept
  • Proactive interference = OLD blocks NEW (not the other way around)
  • Last two units (disorders + treatment) are heavily tested — don't skip them

📚 Core Topics
What's on the Exam
  • Biological Bases of Behavior
    Neurons, neurotransmitters, brain structures, nervous system divisions, endocrine system
  • Sensation & Perception
    Absolute threshold, JND, signal detection, depth cues, visual pathway
  • Learning
    Classical conditioning, operant conditioning (schedules), observational learning (Bandura)
  • Memory
    Encoding → storage → retrieval, explicit vs implicit, hippocampus, interference types
  • Development
    Piaget's 4 stages, Erikson's 8 crises, Kohlberg's moral stages, attachment theory
  • Social Psychology
    Attribution, conformity, obedience, groupthink, bystander effect, cognitive dissonance
  • Disorders & Treatment
    DSM-5 categories, depression, schizophrenia, anxiety, OCD · CBT, SSRIs, psychoanalysis
