AP European History

European history from 1450 to present — Renaissance through the Cold War. Writing skills and historical thinking matter as much as content.

📅 Exam: May ⏱ 3 hr 15 min 🔥 Difficulty: Hard ~55% pass rate
⚡ Exam Tips
  • Contextualization must connect to your argument — mentioning it isn't enough
  • Think in themes: power, religion, economics, culture, social change — not just dates
  • Every MC question has a stimulus — identify the historical context before reading the question
  • DBQ outside evidence = at least one specific fact not in the documents
  • For LEQs: pick the prompt you know best, not the one that sounds easiest

📚 The 4 Major Periods
AP Euro Timeline
  • Period 1 (1450–1648)
    Renaissance, printing press, Protestant Reformation, Scientific Revolution, Age of Exploration, Thirty Years' War
  • Period 2 (1648–1815)
    Absolutism (Louis XIV), Enlightenment, French Revolution phases, Napoleon, Congress of Vienna
  • Period 3 (1815–1914)
    Industrial Revolution, liberalism vs. conservatism vs. socialism, unification of Italy & Germany, new imperialism, WWI buildup
  • Period 4 (1914–Present)
    WWI, Russian Revolution, rise of fascism, WWII & Holocaust, Cold War, European integration, fall of USSR
