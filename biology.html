AP Biology

Cell biology, genetics, evolution, and ecology. Expect data analysis, experimental design, and reasoning — not just memorization.

📅 Exam: May 12 ⏱ 3 hours 🔥 Difficulty: Hard ~65% pass rate
🃏Flashcards ✍️FRQs ▶️Video
📚 Core Topics
What's on the Exam
  • Cell Structure & Function
    Organelles, fluid mosaic membrane model, transport (diffusion, osmosis, active)
  • Cellular Energetics
    Cellular respiration (glycolysis → Krebs → ETC), photosynthesis, enzymes, ATP
  • DNA, Gene Expression & Regulation
    Replication, transcription, translation, operons, mutations, CRISPR, PCR
  • Cell Division & Genetics
    Mitosis, meiosis, Mendelian genetics, non-Mendelian inheritance, chi-square
  • Evolution & Natural Selection
    Hardy-Weinberg, genetic drift, gene flow, speciation, phylogenetics
  • Ecology
    Population dynamics, logistic growth, energy flow, biogeochemical cycles
